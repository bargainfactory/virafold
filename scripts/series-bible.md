**Global strength rules**
- **Primary** = full vignette (7–12 s) + object bridge + named NT verse.
- **Secondary** = short vignette (4–7 s) or strong spoken call-out with the reference.
- **Whisper** = 1–2 spoken lines only. No vignette.
- One Primary maximum per episode.
- Jesus appears only in the elevated golden vignette above the lamp — except Daniel 3, which is an in-scene Christophany under the locked rule below.

## Typology Guardrails

A passage may receive a vignette or strong spoken treatment only if it meets all three:
1. **Historical reality** — the event, person, or institution actually occurred as recorded.
2. **Divine intentionality** — the pattern is designed, not coincidental.
3. **New Testament confirmation** — explicit statement, near-explicit quotation, or clear apostolic use.

If any is missing, the maximum treatment is a spoken whisper. Never invent a vignette to "make it fit."

**Pattern-level confirmation** (Joseph): where the NT uses a figure's *pattern* without naming him a type (Acts 7), the entry may be Primary, but the lesson names the pattern honestly ("the Bible loves to tell this shape of story…") and never claims a verse that isn't there.

### Christophany — a different category entirely (Daniel 3, locked)
A Christophany is a passage where the pre-incarnate Christ is arguably *present in the scene itself*. It does not use the vignette:
- The fourth figure appears **inside the furnace scene** as a light-wrapped presence.
- Face is **never shown**. It is **never** the registered Jesus design.
- The golden vignette above the lamp stays home for this episode.
- The narration quotes the text's own words ("like a son of the gods"); Naomi may whisper the traditional recognition — the show never over-claims what the text leaves in wonder.

### Akedah Safe Framing (locked — non-negotiable)
- Children are pre-assured ("God will provide — He never meant harm") BEFORE the test is fully told.
- No knife, blade, or raised weapon is ever shown or described, visually or verbally.
- The story is framed as trust and provision; the ram is the resolution, never a near-miss.
- The vignette still occurs on the ram (Primary); the preceding scenes stay inside the safe framing.

### Explicit "Never" list — except where the New Testament itself makes the link
A "never" yields only when a New Testament scripture explicitly draws the picture; the exception is then named in the Scarlet Thread table, never improvised on set.
- Never show crucifixion, cross, or resurrection imagery inside an Old Testament vignette — **except the bronze serpent** (Num 21 / John 3:14–15), where Jesus Himself draws the analogy. Treatment for kids: the vignette may mirror the LIFTING — Jesus raised up in golden light, arms open, the lifted pole silhouetted beside Him — never nails, blood, or suffering; the visual rhyme is the lifting and the looking-up-to-live, not the agony.
- Never have Jesus speak dialogue that belongs to the OT characters or advance the OT plot.
- Never imply the OT saints already understood the full New Testament picture.
- Never use the crown of thorns except on clear sacrifice shadows (the ram; the Passover lamb; the Day of Atonement).
- Never place Jesus walking inside the Old Testament scene. He appears only in the golden vignette above the lamp. (Daniel 3's in-scene presence is the text's own light-wrapped figure — never the registered face.)
- Never force a type that fails the three-part test above.

### Writer & director checklist (every shadow episode)
- [ ] Entry exists on the Scarlet Thread table, and the treatment matches its strength.
- [ ] The symbolic object is the clear bridge; nothing else crosses.
- [ ] The exact NT anchor is named out loud.
- [ ] The vignette follows the locked grammar and timing.
- [ ] The historical story resumes cleanly; the shadow served it, not the reverse.

## Locked Visual Templates

### [[Vignette]] grammar
- Elevated golden dream-space above the lamp only. Jesus never enters the OT scene.
- Duration: Primary 7–12 s; Secondary 4–7 s (enforced as narration length — see Engine Mapping).
- Only the approved symbolic object crosses between worlds.
- Registered Jesus face, warm slightly-overexposed gold key, three expressions only: compassionate watchfulness, quiet recognition, quiet invitation. He does not speak.
- Transition (locked): shadow moment → lamp/"look closer" cue → object glows/lifts → vignette opens with object present → NT anchor spoken while it holds → object returns/fades → vignette dissolves upward into the lamp → immediate return to the OT scene.
- Audio: the 4-note motif plays only when the vignette appears; it is never used for anything else.

### [[Thread]] — the Promise Thread (locked)
A thin golden filament with a faint crimson edge: the series' through-line, appearing at promise moments and growing across eras. Never competes with the story; always secondary to the lamp.

Era thickness (identical rendering across all 38 episodes):
| Era | State |
|---|---|
| S1 (first appears E06) | Hair-thin, barely there, flickering |
| S2 Patriarchs | Thin but steady, faintly brighter at covenant moments |
| S3 Exodus–Wilderness | Clearly visible, continuous through the camp |
| S4–S5 Land & Kingdom | A confident unbroken line, warm and calm |
| S6 Toward Exile | Visibly thinner — but NEVER broken |
| S7 Exile & Daniel | Thin in the dark until E38, where it rises blazing and continuous |

## Engine Mapping — how the renderer enforces this bible

### The locked vignette direction template
Every Primary vignette uses THIS direction text, verbatim except the [OBJECT] slot — identical wording is what keeps the vignette face identical, because fireside vignettes are painted from direction text:

```
[[Floating golden dream-vignette glowing in the warm air above the clay lamp: Jesus — deep warm tan, sun-browned Middle-Eastern skin, dark wavy hair and beard, simple cream robes, a calm kind loving face — holding/with [OBJECT], soft golden radiance all around Him; the fireside cast gaze up at it in wonder.]]
```
- Crown of thorns: add "wearing a simple woven crown of thorns" ONLY on sacrifice shadows (rows 4, 8, 12).
- Duration enforced as narration length: Primary = 18–30 spoken words on the carrying line (≈ 7–12 s); Secondary = 10–18 words (≈ 4–7 s).

### The locked Thread direction template
```
[[The Promise Thread: a thin glowing golden filament with a faint crimson edge drifts gently from the lamp toward the horizon — {ERA STATE from the table above} — never touching the characters, always softer than the lamp's own light.]]
```

### Other mappings
- **Registry**: story characters hold one face per life-era across all episodes (story_cast); JESUS consistency in vignettes rides the locked template text above.
- **The 4-note motif**: generated once (data/renders/music/el_motif.mp3), auto-overlaid by the engine on beats whose direction contains both "vignette" and "Jesus" (sfx.ts).
- **Vignette/thought-bubble beats paint fresh** (never cast-sheet img2img — the edit model refuses to add the floating figure).
- **Foley**: the sfx layer reads every story beat and lays cached ElevenLabs sound effects under the mix.
- **QA gate**: every painted still is vision-inspected against the production contract before animation; failures regenerate once.
- **Era hand-offs** (after S2E13, S3E23, S5E31, S7E38): cold-open recap beats using registry faces; the Thread template at that era's thickness.
- **Parent/teacher layer**: each Primary episode's study notes (compiled into the clip at render) carry the OT+NT texts, one sentence on why the type is strong, and one discussion question building on prior shadows.
- **Publish kit**: youtube.txt per film — title, study notes, chapters, disclosures.
