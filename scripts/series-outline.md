## Format & Continuity Notes
- Canonical series = lean 8–12 minute chapters.
- Existing longer Abraham/Isaac/Jacob pieces = extended tellings / deluxe cuts of the same territory.
- New IDs: S1E01 onward. Prior ad-hoc numbering is superseded.
- Lamp liturgy, Psalm 119:105 hook, treasure-box asides, quiz, and memory verse live inside every episode’s structure slot.
- [[Thread]] and [[Vignette]] are locked templates.

## Global Directing Principles
- Historical story leads. Shadow serves the story.
- Lamp is home. [[Thread]] is the through-line.
- Vignettes are rare, earned, and luminous.
- Daniel 3 is the sole in-scene Christophany (light-wrapped, face unseen, not registered Jesus).
- Cold open creates recognition. End card (lamp + Thread) is the feed signature.
- Tone: reverent wonder, warm, clear, child-safe.

## SEASON 1 — BEGINNINGS

### S1E01 — Let There Be
Darkness. Breath. Light breaks. Ordered days. “It was good.” Humanity’s eyes open. First lamp of the series.  
Tease: garden gate.

### S1E02 — The Garden
Walking in the cool of the day. Naming. One tree, one word. Quiet contentment.  
Tease: soft-focus serpent.

### S1E03 — The First Promise
The taking. Shame. God walks and asks. Genesis 3:15 spoken.  
**Primary vignette**: promise light rises into [[Vignette]].  
They leave; the light remains in the lamp.  
Tease: two brothers.

### S1E04 — Two Brothers
Two offerings. Field (restraint). Mark of mercy. Sorrow and kindness.  
Tease: a darkening world.

### S1E05 — The Ark
Violence. One man walks with God. Door closes. Rain. Dry ground.  
**Secondary**: ark door / rainbow.  
Tease: a tower.

### S1E06 — The Tower and the Promise
Proud plan. Scattering. A family begins to be called.  
**[[Thread]] first appearance** — very thin, locked template.  
Tease: “Leave your country.”

## SEASON 2 — THE PATRIARCHS

### S2E07 — Leave Your Country
Abraham hears and goes. Stars. Covenant. Thread faint among the stars.  
Tease: bread and cup.

### S2E08 — Bread and Cup
After battle. Melchizedek. Bread and cup shared.  
**Primary vignette**: bread and cup.  
Tease: laughter in a tent.

### S2E09 — The Laughter of Promise
Waiting. The child is born. Sarah laughs. Promise has a face.  
Tease: a mountain.

### S2E10 — The Ram in the Thicket
**Safe framing locked**: children pre-assured God will provide. No knife or weapon shown at any point.  
Journey. “Where is the lamb?” Provision revealed as the ram in the thicket.  
**Primary vignette**: ram (only thorn imagery allowed).  
They worship and return.  
Tease: a stone pillow.

### S2E11 — The Ladder
Jacob alone. Stone pillow. Ladder and promise.  
**Primary vignette**: ladder of light.  
“Surely the Lord is in this place.”  
Tease: a colorful coat.

### S2E12 — The Dreamer
Dreams. Jealousy. Pit and sale (restraint). Jacob mourns.  
Tease: Egypt.

### S2E13 — From Pit to Palace
Exaltation. Brothers bow. “You meant evil… God meant it for good.”  
**Primary vignette**: coat → royal robe.  
Family restored.  
**Hand-off 1**: The Promise Leaves the Tents.

## SEASON 3 — EXODUS & WILDERNESS

### S3E14 — The Bush That Burns
Ordinary day. Bush burns, is not consumed. “I AM.”  
Whisper: presence.  
Tease: “Let my people go.”

### S3E15 — Let My People Go
Confrontation. Selected plagues with dignity, not spectacle. Final warning.  
Tease: one lamb per house.

### S3E16 — The Night of the Lamb
Spotless lamb. Blood on the door (stylized). Waiting.  
**Primary vignette**: lamb / door.  
They leave under the pillar.  
Tease: the sea.

### S3E17 — Through the Sea
Trapped. Waters part. Crossing. Song on the far shore.  
Tease: thirst.

### S3E18 — Bread from Heaven
Hunger. Manna.  
**Secondary vignette**: bread from heaven.  
Tease: a dry rock.

### S3E19 — Water from the Rock
No water. Rock struck. Water flows.  
**Primary vignette**: water from the rock.  
Tease: thunder on a mountain.

### S3E20 — The Mountain and the Law
Boundaries. Ten words given clearly. Mediator.  
Tease: a pattern for a tent.

### S3E21 — The Tent of Meeting
Built with care. Glory fills it.  
**Primary vignette**: lampstand / veil (John 1:14).  
Tease: one day a year.

### S3E22 — The Day of Covering
High priest. Covering explained simply. Scapegoat sent away.  
**Primary vignette**: mercy seat / scapegoat (Hebrews 9–10).  
Tease: serpents.

### S3E23 — The Lifted Serpent
Complaint. Serpents. Bronze serpent lifted. Looking brings life.  
**Primary vignette**: lifted serpent.  
**Hand-off 2**: The Promise Crosses the River.

## SEASON 6 — TOWARD EXILE

### S6E32 — The Kingdom Divides
Rehoboam. Two kingdoms. Thread continues, thinner but unbroken.  
Tease: voices calling back.

### S6E33 — Prophets in the Land
One clear prophetic moment. Fire or word.  
Tease: a sign still to come.

### S6E33.5 — The Sign of Jonah
Jonah called. Flight. Storm. Great fish. Three days. Repentance in Nineveh.

### S7E35 — Faithful in Babylon
New names. Resolve. Favor and wisdom.

**Hand-off 4**: Every Shadow Was Waiting.
