# ========== CAST PROFILES — scripture-grounded production notes (never spoken) ==========
# CAST — JACOB: Physical — a lean, smooth-skinned man (Gen 27:11) in his prime years, sharp intelligent eyes, dark hair and short beard, simple traveler's tunic and cloak with a long walking staff he is never without (Gen 32:10 — "with only my staff I crossed this Jordan"); after the river night he walks with a limp (Gen 32:31). Personality — a quiet tent-dweller (Gen 25:27) whose cleverness starts as scheming and is slowly refined into persistence; loves so deeply that seven years seem like days (Gen 29:20); will not let go until blessed (Gen 32:26); learns to bow low and go first toward the brother he wronged (Gen 33:3). Aliases — "the younger twin", "the heel-grabber", "her son", "the trickster".
# CAST — ESAU: Physical — a big powerful man, ruddy-red coloring with thick hair like a garment on his arms (Gen 25:25), full wild beard, a skillful hunter's leathers and bow, a man of the open field (Gen 25:27). Personality — impulsive and appetite-driven in youth (Gen 25:32-34), hot in anger — but with a huge forgiving heart at the last: he RUNS to his brother, embraces him, and weeps (Gen 33:4). Aliases — "the older twin", "the hunter", "his brother".
# CAST — RACHEL: Physical — a young shepherdess, beautiful in form and appearance (Gen 29:17), dark braided hair under a simple head-scarf, practical robes of a working shepherd girl, often with a lamb at her feet (Gen 29:9 — she kept her father's sheep). Personality — capable and steady, at home among flocks and wells. Aliases — "the shepherdess", "his beloved".
# CAST — LEAH: Physical — Rachel's older sister, a gentle-faced young woman with soft delicate eyes (Gen 29:17), long dark hair in a simple braid, modest robes in muted blues and cream. Personality — overlooked but deeply faithful; her heart grows from striving for love to simply praising God (Gen 29:35 — "This time I will praise the Lord"); mother of six of the twelve tribes, among them Levi and Judah. Aliases — "the older sister", "the unloved one".
# GROUP — ESAU'S FOUR HUNDRED MEN: a long column of rugged armed men of the field stretched across the hills (Gen 33:1), loyal and weathered, not an army in armor.
# PROP — THE STONE PILLOW: one smooth field stone the size of a lamb, set upright afterward as a pillar and anointed with oil (Gen 28:18).
# PROP — THE LADDER OF LIGHT: a blazing golden stairway reaching from the ground beside the sleeper into the highest stars, messengers of light ascending and descending (Gen 28:12); the Lord above it seen ONLY as glory-light (Gen 28:13).
# PROP — THE RED STEW: a steaming clay pot of deep red lentil stew (Gen 25:30) over a small cook-fire.
# NOTE — GOD: never depicted as a person or figure; His presence is radiant light and the Voice alone.
