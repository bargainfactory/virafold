# S1E01 — Let There Be
# PROMISE: You will watch the whole world arrive at the sound of one Voice — and learn that the same Voice knows your name.
# REPEATABLE — "God said — and it was!"
# ARC — WANT: Eli wants to know how anything began | NEED: to trust that what God speaks, happens | TURN: the first light breaking the dark | TEAR-BEAT: "and He was not far from anyone" over humanity's first seeing (~70%)
# CAST — ADAM: Physical — a strong young man in his first days, warm deep-tan skin, thick dark curly hair and short beard, simple garment of woven leaves, bright unguarded eyes. Personality — wonder-filled namer of creatures (Gen 2:19-20); works the garden with joy (Gen 2:15). Aliases — "the man", "the first man".
# CAST — EVE: Physical — a beautiful young woman, warm tan skin, long flowing dark hair, graceful and strong, garment of woven leaves, eyes quick with delight. Personality — curious and brave-hearted; the mother of all living (Gen 3:20). Aliases — "the woman", "the mother of all living".
# PLACE — THE LAMP ROOM: a cozy ancient night-time home interior, warm stone walls, a woven rug, a single glowing clay oil lamp on a low round stone table at center, soft hearth-glow at one side, deep indigo night through one small arched window.
# NOTE — GOD: never depicted; warm light and the Voice alone.

NARRATOR: [[Palette: deepest indigo into gold. Three-second cold open: total darkness — then one slow breath of wind across the frame, and a thin seam of light splits the black from edge to edge; the light blooms outward like living brushstrokes.]] Before anything... there was nothing at all. No sky above, no ground below, no yesterday behind. And then — a breath. And a Voice.
VOICE(atlas): Let there be light.
NARRATOR: [[The newborn light washes the whole frame gold; darkness rolls back like a curtain drawn by unseen hands.]] And there WAS light. Just like that. No hammer, no helper, no waiting. He spoke — and it was.
NAOMI: Little ones! The stars are out, the night is soft... so tell me — who lights the lamp?
ELI: WE light the lamp!
PIP: Ooooh... watch it glow!
NAOMI: Then gather close, lamplighters — because tonight is the very first story there ever was. The beginning of beginnings. Sing it with me!
ELI: Your word is a lamp for my feet!
PIP: A light for my path so sweet!
ELI: When the road is dark and long —
PIP: Light the lamp and sing along!
NAOMI: Little ones, every story you will ever hear by this lamp grows out of tonight's. So hold your flame steady... and watch the world arrive.
NARRATOR: [[Palette: gold and deep blue. Wide cosmic shot: light and darkness gently parted like two great rooms; evening settles, morning rises.]] God separated the light from the darkness. He called the light Day, and the darkness Night. And there was evening, and there was morning — the first day.
ELI: [curious] But Naomi... how do we know the light STAYS?
NAOMI: Because the One who spoke it never stopped holding it, Eli. He doesn't just make things — He KEEPS them. Watch.
PIP: [awe] He NAMED them? Like... like naming a lamb?
NAOMI: Just like that, little lamb. In those days — and this is your treasure-box fact for tonight — to NAME something was to care for it, to say "you are mine and I know you." Remember that. It matters at the end of tonight's story.
NARRATOR: [[A vast sky-dome arches up out of the waters, blue above and blue below, clouds drifting newborn.]] On the second day He stretched out the sky like a tent over the deep. And on the third —
VOICE(atlas): Let the dry land appear. Let the earth sprout grass, and trees bearing fruit, each with its seed.
NARRATOR: [[The land rose up from the sea, green unrolling across the hills like a carpet flung wide; orchards bloomed into being, one continuous flowering reveal.]] — the land rose up, and the whole earth went green at His word. Apple and olive, cedar and fig, grass unrolling to the horizon. And God saw that it was good.
ELI: [excited] It just... GREW? All at once, because He said so?
NAOMI: Because He said so, Eli. Mark tonight's one great secret: when God says it... it HAPPENS. His words don't just tell about things — they MAKE them.
NARRATOR: [[Palette: velvet night sky. The sun blazing gentle and gold; the moon rising silver; stars sown across the dark like seed flung from a hand.]] On the fourth day He hung the sun to rule the day, and the moon to rule the night — and the stars. Oh, little ones, the stars. He made them too, every single one.
PIP: [giggles] A whole sky full of little lamps!
NAOMI: And not one of them ever runs out of oil, little lamb.
NARRATOR: [[Teeming shallows flashing with fish; the deep rolling with great whales; the air filling with wings — sparrows, herons, eagles turning in the gold light.]] On the fifth day the waters swarmed and the sky sang: fish silvering the seas, great whales rolling in the deep, and birds — every feather and song of them.
NARRATOR: [[The plains filling with creatures: lions and lambs, deer and bears, oxen and little scurrying things; among them, wonderfully, great long-necked and horned creatures of old walking the same young earth.]] And on the sixth day came the animals of the land — the gentle and the great, the swift and the slow, creatures we still know and mighty creatures of long ago — all of them His idea, all of them good.
PIP: [excited] The BIG ones too? The long-neck ones?
NAOMI: Every one that ever walked, little lamb. His imagination is bigger than our books.
PIP: [giggles] I'd need a ladder just to say hello to its KNEES!
NARRATOR: [[Hold a quiet beat: the whole young world at dusk, waiting; the light gathers softly in one place like held breath.]] And then the world went quiet... because the best was last. God said: let us make mankind in OUR image.
NARRATOR: [[Close-up, tender and unhurried: Adam's eyes opening for the first time, gold evening light on his face; reverse: Eve's first smile, taking in the whole garden at once.]] And He made them — the man and the woman — made in the image of God Himself. Watch their eyes open. The first seeing. The first breath. The first laugh at a butterfly.
ELI: [softly] He wasn't far from anyone... was He.
NAOMI: [tender] Not from anyone, Eli. Carry that to bed, little ones: of everything God made — seas and stars and singing birds — only PEOPLE were made like Him, to know Him and walk with Him. Not far then... and not far now.
ELI: So when God made everything... He was already thinking about US?
NAOMI: About you, Eli. About Pip. About every little one listening by every lamp. You were the point of the sixth day.
NARRATOR: [[The seventh-day light: the whole creation at rest under a wide golden calm; nothing moves but slow clouds and a drifting leaf.]] And on the seventh day, God rested — not because He was tired, but the way a father sits back to enjoy what he loves. He blessed that day and made it holy.
ELI: He spoke and the light came through —
PIP: He spoke and the mountains grew!
ELI: He spoke and the sparrows flew —
PIP: and then, at last... He thought of YOU!
NAOMI: So, little ones — tonight's one clear thing: God's word MAKES things. When He speaks, it happens. Hold on to that, because every promise He ever makes in all our stories rests on it: the One who said "let there be light" and got light... keeps His word.
NAOMI: And your treasure from earlier — He NAMES what He loves. He named the Day and the Night... and He knows YOUR name too.
NAOMI: Quiz time, lamplighters! What did God use to make the whole world — a hammer, helpers, or His word? Shout it out!
PIP: His WORD! He said it and it WAS!
NAOMI: And what was the very last, very best thing He made? Shout it out!
ELI: PEOPLE! Made in His image — made like Him!
NAOMI: Lamp down low. Say it slow. So your heart will always know.
NAOMI: He spoke, and it came to be; He commanded, and it stood firm. Psalm thirty-three, verse nine.
ELI: He spoke, and it came to be.
PIP: And it stood firm!
ELI: Your word is a lamp for my feet —
PIP: a light for my path so sweet!
ELI: Follow the light!
PIP: Follow the light!
NAOMI: Next time, little ones — a garden with a gate, where God Himself comes walking in the cool of the day... and one tree with one word on it. Come and walk with us.
NARRATOR: [[End card grammar: the lamp close and warm, the night deep behind it.]] Follow for the next lamplight story.
