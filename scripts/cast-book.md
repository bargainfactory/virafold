# Cast Book — Canonical Character Profiles (Creation → Daniel)

Every recurring character in the 38-episode arc, profiled ONCE. Episode scripts
copy the relevant `# CAST` blocks verbatim from here; the Story Cast Registry
pins each face at first render and reuses it forever (era-split where a life
spans decades). JESUS is not cast here — his appearance is locked in the
series bible's vignette template. GOD is never depicted: warm light and the
Voice alone.

Already registered from prior renders: ABRAHAM (era ~100), ISAAC (era ~75).
The fireside trio (Naomi, Eli, Pip) are platform personas, not story cast.

## Season 1 — Beginnings

# CAST — ADAM: Physical — a strong young man in his first days, warm deep-tan skin, thick dark curly hair and short beard, simple garment of woven leaves then plain skins (Gen 3:21), bright unguarded eyes. Personality — wonder-filled namer of creatures (Gen 2:19-20); works the garden with joy (Gen 2:15); after the fall, carries a quieter, humbled hope. Aliases — "the man", "the first man".
# CAST — EVE: Physical — a beautiful young woman, warm tan skin, long flowing dark hair, graceful and strong, garment of leaves then skins, eyes quick with delight. Personality — curious and brave-hearted; the mother of all living (Gen 3:20); her sorrow after the fall is carried with dignity, never shame-faced caricature. Aliases — "the woman", "the mother of all living".
# CAST — THE SERPENT: Physical — before the curse, a gleaming elegant creature of burnished copper-gold scales moving upright among the branches; rendered beautiful and subtly wrong, never horror. Personality — smooth, flattering, question-twisting (Gen 3:1 — "Did God really say…?"). Aliases — "the serpent", "the snake".
# CAST — CAIN: Physical — a broad, strong young farmer, sun-browned, heavy brows, earth-stained tunic, strong hands. Personality — works hard but holds back his best; anger falls on his face (Gen 4:5); God's warning finds him at the door. Aliases — "the older brother", "the farmer".
# CAST — ABEL: Physical — a lean gentle young shepherd, open face, simple light tunic, crook in hand, often with a lamb. Personality — gives God his first and best (Gen 4:4); quiet, unguarded. Aliases — "the younger brother", "the shepherd".
# CAST — NOAH: Physical — a weathered, sturdy old man of great age, long white beard, bald crown with wisps, rope-belted work robes, hands scarred by honest building. Personality — walks with God when no one else does (Gen 6:9); obeys exactly (Gen 6:22); patient through decades of mockery; first act on dry ground is worship (Gen 8:20). Aliases — "the ark builder", "the old builder".
# GROUP — NOAH'S FAMILY: his wife and three sons with their wives — eight weathered, kind-faced workers in earth-tone robes and head-wraps, always working together (Gen 7:13).

## Season 2 — The Patriarchs (Abraham, Sarah, Isaac, Rebekah, Jacob, Esau, Rachel, Leah, Melchizedek: profiles already in ep13–ep15 scripts; registry holds the rendered faces)

# CAST — JOSEPH (the young dreamer): Physical — a bright-eyed youth of seventeen (Gen 37:2), smooth-faced, dark curls, wearing the famous richly ornamented coat of many colors (Gen 37:3) over a cream tunic. Personality — earnest and guileless to a fault; tells his dreams plainly; keeps integrity in every house he enters (Gen 39:9). Aliases — "the dreamer", "the favorite son", "the boy with the coat".
# CAST — JOSEPH (lord of Egypt): Physical — a commanding man of about forty, clean-shaven in the Egyptian manner (Gen 41:14), kohl-lined eyes, white linen robes with a gold collar and signet ring (Gen 41:42), Pharaoh's second chariot behind him. Personality — wise administrator; weeps easily and privately for his brothers (Gen 42:24, 45:2); forgives with the great sentence "you meant evil against me, but God meant it for good" (Gen 50:20). Aliases — "the governor", "the lord of the land", "Zaphenath-Paneah".
# GROUP — JOSEPH'S BROTHERS: ten weathered shepherd brothers in rough earth-tone robes and head-cloths, ranging middle-aged, led in speech by Judah; later joined by young Benjamin, the smallest, in a clean pale tunic.

## Season 3 — Exodus & Wilderness

# CAST — MOSES: Physical — a mighty old man of eighty (Ex 7:7), long grey-white beard, sun-darkened craggy face, simple shepherd's robe over one shoulder, ALWAYS carrying the wooden staff of God (Ex 4:20); after Sinai his face carries a faint lingering radiance (Ex 34:29). Personality — slow of speech and sure of God (Ex 4:10); the meekest man on earth (Num 12:3) yet fearless before Pharaoh; intercedes for the people even when they rage at him. Aliases — "the deliverer", "the man of God", "the shepherd of Israel".
# CAST — AARON: Physical — Moses' older brother, eighty-three (Ex 7:7), fuller silver beard, kindlier rounder face; in priestly scenes the high-priest's vestments — blue robe, ephod, breastpiece with twelve stones, gold plate on the turban (Ex 28). Personality — the spokesman (Ex 4:14-16); warm, sometimes swayed by the crowd; bears the people's names over his heart. Aliases — "the brother", "the high priest", "the priest".
# CAST — MIRIAM: Physical — the elder sister, a dignified old woman, silver-braided hair under a striped head-scarf, a timbrel often in hand (Ex 15:20). Personality — the prophetess who leads the women in the song of the sea; watchful since the river basket (Ex 2:4). Aliases — "the sister", "the prophetess".
# CAST — PHARAOH: Physical — a powerful Egyptian king, shaved head under the striped nemes crown with the cobra, kohl-lined hard eyes, broad gold collar, white linen; throne room of lotus columns. Personality — proud and hard-hearted (Ex 7:13); grief cracks him only for a night; majesty worn as armor. Aliases — "the king of Egypt".
# CAST — JOSHUA (the aide): Physical — a strong watchful man in his prime, short dark beard, soldier's leather over a plain tunic, always near Moses (Ex 33:11). Personality — whole-hearted (Num 14:24); waits at the tent while others wander. Aliases — "the aide", "Moses' helper".

## Season 4 — Conquest, Judges & Ruth

# CAST — JOSHUA (the leader): Physical — now a grey-bearded commander of about eighty, weathered and upright, leather and bronze over his robe, the scroll of the Law never far (Josh 1:8). Personality — strong and courageous on command (Josh 1:9); divides the land faithfully; "as for me and my house" (Josh 24:15). Aliases — "the leader", "the commander", "son of Nun".
# CAST — RAHAB: Physical — a quick-eyed woman of Jericho in layered city robes of faded red and ochre, dark hair bound in a patterned scarf, the SCARLET CORD at her window (Josh 2:21). Personality — shrewd and brave; stakes everything on the God she has only heard of (Josh 2:11); gathers her whole family under one roof. Aliases — "the woman of Jericho", "the woman with the cord".
# CAST — DEBORAH: Physical — a stately judge-prophetess, silver-streaked dark hair, seated beneath her palm (Judg 4:5), robes of deep blue, a mother-in-Israel's steady gaze. Personality — decisive, fearless, poetic (Judg 5); sends and goes. Aliases — "the judge", "the prophetess", "a mother in Israel".
# CAST — GIDEON: Physical — a wiry young farmer threshing wheat in a winepress (Judg 6:11), rough tunic, doubtful eyes that learn to burn; later a torch-and-trumpet night commander. Personality — begins fearful and fleece-testing; obeys anyway; three hundred against a host. Aliases — "the young farmer", "mighty warrior".
# CAST — RUTH: Physical — a young Moabite widow, sun-browned from gleaning, dark braid under a plain head-scarf, patched travel cloak, a sheaf of barley often in her arms (Ruth 2:17). Personality — loyalty itself ("where you go I will go" — Ruth 1:16); humble, industrious, brave at the threshing floor. Aliases — "the Moabitess", "the gleaner", "the daughter-in-law".
# CAST — NAOMI OF BETHLEHEM: Physical — an aging widow, grief-lined but gentle face, grey hair under a dark mourning veil that later brightens, worn travel robes. Personality — honest about bitterness ("call me Mara" — Ruth 1:20); schemes only for Ruth's good; ends with a grandson on her lap (Ruth 4:16). Aliases — "the mother-in-law".
# CAST — BOAZ: Physical — a solid landowner in his strong middle years, trimmed dark-grey beard, fine but unshowy robes with a redeemer's cloak, standing tall among his reapers (Ruth 2:4). Personality — greets workers with blessing; protects before he is asked; does the kinsman's duty publicly at the gate (Ruth 4). Aliases — "the kinsman", "the redeemer", "the landowner".

## Season 5 — United Kingdom

# CAST — SAMUEL: Physical — an old prophet-judge, long white hair and beard, simple robe with the little remembered coat motif (1 Sam 2:19), a horn of oil at his belt (1 Sam 16:1). Personality — hears and answers "speak, for your servant is listening" since boyhood (1 Sam 3:10); looks on the heart because God does (1 Sam 16:7). Aliases — "the prophet", "the seer".
# CAST — DAVID (the shepherd boy): Physical — a ruddy, bright-eyed youth (1 Sam 16:12), windblown auburn-brown curls, simple shepherd's tunic with a sling at his belt and a lyre on his back. Personality — fearless from the pasture (lion and bear — 1 Sam 17:36); sings to the Lord; fights in God's name, not armor (1 Sam 17:45). Aliases — "the shepherd boy", "the youngest son", "the boy with the sling".
# CAST — DAVID (the king): Physical — a strong king in his prime-to-grey years, trimmed beard going silver, simple crown over auburn-grey hair, royal robes of deep red worn plainly, the lyre still near the throne. Personality — a man after God's own heart (1 Sam 13:14); dances before the ark (2 Sam 6:14); prays like a shepherd even on a throne (2 Sam 7:18). Aliases — "the king", "the shepherd king".
# CAST — NATHAN: Physical — a plain-robed court prophet, keen gentle eyes, dark beard streaked grey, staff in hand. Personality — carries God's word to the king's face, promise and rebuke alike (2 Sam 7; 12). Aliases — "the prophet Nathan".
# CAST — SOLOMON: Physical — a composed young king, smooth dark beard, gold-edged white robes, rings and wisdom scrolls, the temple plans often in hand. Personality — asks for a listening heart above riches (1 Kgs 3:9); builds with care; kneels before the cloud-filled house (1 Kgs 8:22). Aliases — "the wise king", "David's son".

## Seasons 6–7 — Prophets, Exile & Daniel

# CAST — ELIJAH: Physical — a wild-haired prophet of the hills, garment of hair with a leather belt (2 Kgs 1:8), wind-burned face, fierce kind eyes. Personality — stands alone against hundreds (1 Kgs 18:22); runs on God's strength; hears the low whisper after the fire (1 Kgs 19:12). Aliases — "the prophet of fire", "the Tishbite".
# CAST — JONAH: Physical — a stocky, storm-tossed prophet, tangled dark beard, salt-stained robes, often dripping or sun-scorched. Personality — runs the wrong way first (Jonah 1:3); prays from the deep (Jonah 2); obeys the second call; sulks under the vine while God teaches mercy (Jonah 4). Aliases — "the runaway prophet", "the prophet in the fish".
# CAST — DANIEL (the youth): Physical — a noble Judean teenager in Babylonian court dress worn modestly, clear thoughtful eyes, dark hair neatly kept. Personality — resolves not to defile himself (Dan 1:8); courteous to guards and kings alike; wisdom ten times beyond his class (Dan 1:20). Aliases — "the young exile", "Belteshazzar".
# CAST — DANIEL (the elder statesman): Physical — a silver-bearded, straight-backed old counselor in fine Persian-court robes worn simply, windows open toward Jerusalem (Dan 6:10). Personality — prays three times daily regardless of decrees; reads walls kings fear; keeps visions humbly in his heart (Dan 7:28). Aliases — "the counselor", "the man greatly loved".
# GROUP — THE THREE FRIENDS: Hananiah, Mishael and Azariah — three steadfast young Judeans in matching Babylonian court tunics, standing shoulder to shoulder; faces resolute and calm ("our God is able… but even if not" — Dan 3:17-18). Aliases — "Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego", "the three".
# CAST — NEBUCHADNEZZAR: Physical — the towering king of Babylon, square oiled black beard in ringlets, gold-crusted blue robes, ziggurat crown. Personality — thunder and pride, dreams that terrify him; learns by degrees that Heaven rules (Dan 4:34-37). Aliases — "the king of Babylon".

## Registry rules reminder
- charname = the ALL-CAPS name above; era is derived from the stated age, so DAVID the boy and DAVID the king register separately (as do Joseph's and Daniel's two eras).
- Aliases are episode-local for casting; cross-episode recall matches charname only.
- NAOMI OF BETHLEHEM is deliberately NOT named plain "Naomi" to avoid colliding with the fireside storyteller.

## Season 2 — The New Testament (S2E01–S2E15)

Already registered and REUSED: MOSES and ELIJAH (the Transfiguration, S2E10,
uses their Old Testament season faces — the registry's finest hour). JESUS'
design is the bible's locked template, registered at S2E01; engine-enforced:
He is never auto-recalled by name-match.

Each character carries a # PIXAR note — Want/Need arc, leitmotif, silhouette
signature, home plate, and the Brain-Trust watch (the failure mode critics
must guard against for this character).

# CAST — JESUS: Physical — (the locked template, verbatim) a man with deep warm tan, sun-browned Middle-Eastern skin, long dark wavy hair and beard, simple cream robes, a calm kind loving face; sturdy carpenter's hands (Mark 6:3). Personality — speaks ONLY what the Gospels record (red-letter discipline); welcomes children (Mark 10:14); weeps openly (John 11:35); fierce only against hardness of heart; sets His face toward Jerusalem (Luke 9:51). Aliases — NONE registered (engine rule: explicit appearances only, never name-match recall).
# PIXAR — JESUS: Want — to gather and teach His people | Need — (He alone has none to learn; the NEED belongs to everyone around Him — write every episode so another character carries the arc) | Leitmotif — the 4-note shadow motif itself, resolved into a full warm phrase: the sound the series taught now belongs to Him | Silhouette — open arms, slightly bowed head; readable at any distance | Home plate — the Galilee shore | Trust-watch — invented dialogue; cuteness; ever being merely "nice" instead of good.

# CAST — MARY (of Nazareth, the Annunciation): Physical — a young woman of about sixteen, warm tan skin, dark hair braided under a simple blue-grey veil, plain homespun robes of Nazareth poverty, steady wondering eyes. Personality — "let it be to me according to your word" (Luke 1:38); ponders and treasures in her heart (Luke 2:19); sings the Magnificat — a poet's soul in a peasant girl. Aliases — "the mother", "the girl of Nazareth".
# CAST — MARY (the mother, ministry years): Physical — the same face aged to about forty-five, lines of long treasuring, the same blue-grey veil now faded, dignified and quiet. Personality — points others to Him ("do whatever He tells you" — John 2:5); stands at the cross when nearly all have fled (John 19:25). Aliases — "His mother".
# PIXAR — MARY: Want — to protect her son | Need — to release Him to be who He is | Leitmotif — a single soft lyre line, rising (the Magnificat seed) | Silhouette — hands folded over heart, head inclined | Home plate — the Nazareth courtyard | Trust-watch — porcelain-saint stiffness; let her be a real mother who laughs and fears.

# CAST — JOSEPH OF NAZARETH: Physical — a sturdy young carpenter, dark curly hair and short beard, sawdust-flecked work tunic, strong careful hands, kind tired eyes. Personality — a just man who protects quietly (Matt 1:19); obeys dreams at once, by night (Matt 2:14); teaches a trade. Aliases — "the carpenter".
# PIXAR — JOSEPH OF NAZARETH: Want — a quiet righteous life | Need — the courage to shelter a scandal that is really a glory | Leitmotif — low wooden flute, few notes, steady | Silhouette — a guardian's stance, one hand on a shoulder | Home plate — the carpenter's shop | Trust-watch — he vanishes into the background; give him one strong beat per appearance.

# CAST — GABRIEL: Physical — a tall messenger of light — humanlike form wrapped in soft white-gold radiance, his FACE MADE OF PURE LIGHT: a gentle warm brightness where features would be, no eyes, nose or mouth ever drawn, light trailing like slow wind. Personality — begins every message with "Do not be afraid" (Luke 1:30); stands in the presence of God (Luke 1:19); joy barely contained. Aliases — "the angel", "the messenger".
# PIXAR — GABRIEL: Want — to deliver the news well | Need — none; he is the season's pure joy-carrier | Leitmotif — the highest three notes of the shadow motif, like bells far away | Silhouette — arrival-in-light, one raised open hand | Home plate — none (he arrives) | Trust-watch — horror-glow or greeting-card kitsch; keep him strange AND warm.

# CAST — JOHN THE BAPTIST: Physical — a lean sun-scorched wilderness prophet, wild dark hair, camel-hair garment with a leather belt (Matt 3:4), river-wet to the knees, eyes like a desert hawk. Personality — all arrow, no self: "He must increase, I must decrease" (John 3:30); fearless before crowds and kings; tender the moment he sees the Lamb (John 1:29). Aliases — "the Baptizer", "the voice in the wilderness".
# PIXAR — JOHN THE BAPTIST: Want — to wake the people | Need — to step back when the One he announced arrives | Leitmotif — a raw single horn note over river sounds | Silhouette — pointing away from himself | Home plate — the Jordan ford | Trust-watch — ranting caricature; his fire is love with its sleeves rolled up.

# CAST — PETER: Physical — a barrel-chested Galilean fisherman in his thirties, curly dark hair and full beard, net-calloused hands, patched working tunic always half-wet. Personality — first to speak, first to leap, first to sink (Matt 14:29-30); boasts, then breaks, then is restored over breakfast coals (John 21); the rock who learns rock-ness slowly. Aliases — "Simon", "the fisherman", "the big fisherman".
# PIXAR — PETER: Want — to be the bravest one | Need — to let grace hold him after he fails | Leitmotif — hearty low strings with a stumble-step rhythm | Silhouette — leaning forward, mid-stride, always first | Home plate — the fishing boat | Trust-watch — comic-relief creep; his humor must never cheapen his courage or his tears.

# CAST — JOHN THE BELOVED: Physical — the youngest disciple, late teens, smooth-faced with dark curls, quick observant eyes, a listener's stillness beside Peter's storm. Personality — leans close at the supper (John 13:25); stays at the cross and receives a mother (John 19:26-27); runs faster but waits at the tomb door (John 20:4-5). Aliases — "the beloved disciple", "the young one".
# PIXAR — JOHN THE BELOVED: Want — to stay near Him | Need — to become a witness who tells it | Leitmotif — a soft kalimba pattern, like small waves | Silhouette — close at the shoulder, looking up | Home plate — none (he is wherever Jesus is) | Trust-watch — passivity; staying near is his ACTION, shoot it as one.

# CAST — JUDAS: Physical — a neat, capable man, trimmed beard, the money bag at his belt (John 12:6) worn like a responsibility, eyes that stop meeting others as the season darkens. Personality — competent, trusted, disappointed; chooses wrongly and is swallowed by sorrow (Matt 27:3); written as a friend who fell, never a monster. Aliases — "the treasurer".
# PIXAR — JUDAS: Want — the kingdom on his own timetable | Need — (tragically refused) to bring his disappointment into the light | Leitmotif — Peter's string theme a half-step flat, trailing off unresolved | Silhouette — half-turned away from the group | Home plate — the edge of every gathering | Trust-watch — THE care rule: sorrow not villainy; no sinister lighting, no serpent cues; his end is grieved off-screen.

# CAST — MARY MAGDALENE: Physical — a woman in her thirties, long dark hair under a deep-red scarf, a face that has known darkness and now cannot stop catching light, travel-worn robes. Personality — delivered and devoted (Luke 8:2); last at the cross, first at the tomb (John 20:1); the first witness — "I have seen the Lord" (John 20:18). Aliases — "the Magdalene".
# PIXAR — MARY MAGDALENE: Want — to serve the One who freed her | Need — to become the first herald, not only a follower | Leitmotif — a bright harp arpeggio that begins minor and ends major | Silhouette — running with news | Home plate — the tomb garden (hers is the dawn there) | Trust-watch — reducing her to tears-only; her defining frame is the RUN.

# CAST — MARTHA: Physical — a capable householder of Bethany, sleeves rolled, keys and cloths at her belt, strong warm face. Personality — serves until she bursts (Luke 10:40); then speaks one of the Gospels' greatest confessions: "You are the Christ" (John 11:27). Aliases — "the sister of Bethany".
# CAST — LAZARUS: Physical — a gentle householder, short dark beard, plain good robes; in the sign scene, grave-wrapped in white linen, then unbound and blinking at the light. Personality — the quiet friend whose silence after the tomb says everything (John 12:2 — he simply sits at table with Him). Aliases — "the friend of Jesus", "the man from the tomb".
# PIXAR — BETHANY HOUSE (Martha, Mary of Bethany, Lazarus): Want — to host Him well | Need — to receive what only He can give | Leitmotif — domestic strings warming to one swelling line at "come out" | Silhouette — Martha mid-task; Lazarus at the tomb mouth, light behind | Home plate — the Bethany courtyard and tomb | Trust-watch — the raising played as spectacle; the shot is faces, then the unbinding.

# CAST — PILATE: Physical — a Roman governor in a white toga with a purple stripe, short military haircut, a basin never far from reach, tired political eyes. Personality — finds no fault yet yields (John 19:4-16); washes his hands of what his word could have stopped (Matt 27:24) — the image of avoidance, not a villain. Aliases — "the governor".
# CAST — THE HIGH PRIEST: Physical — an imposing figure in the high-priestly vestments worn as armor, grey beard, guarded eyes. Personality — fear of losing nation and place (John 11:48) dressed as zeal; a specific leader acting from fear and pride — NEVER a people. Aliases — "Caiaphas".
# PIXAR — THE AUTHORITIES (Pilate, the High Priest): Want — order kept, position kept | Need — the truth standing right in front of them | Leitmotif — none (their scenes get silence where music would be) | Silhouette — seated power confronted by a standing prisoner | Home plate — the judgment pavement | Trust-watch — the series' heaviest care rule: no caricature, no us/them framing; everyone's fear is recognizably human.

# CAST — SATAN (the tempter): Physical — never fully seen: a voice, a long shadow at the frame's edge, sand lifting where no wind is. Personality — quotes scripture smoothly out of shape (Matt 4:6); offers shortcuts; departs "until an opportune time" (Luke 4:13). Aliases — "the tempter", "the enemy".
# PIXAR — SATAN: Want — one bow | Need — none; he is pressure, not a person who grows | Leitmotif — the absence of the Thread's shimmer (the mix goes dry) | Silhouette — the shadow across the path, never a body | Home plate — the wilderness | Trust-watch — monster-creep; the scarier and truer choice is smoothness.

# GROUP — THE TWELVE: twelve Galilean working men in earth-tone robes and sashes, distinct silhouettes (tall, short, broad, young), always framed as a traveling household around one center; Peter, John and Judas carry the featured faces.
# GROUP — THE SHEPHERDS OF BETHLEHEM: night-watch shepherds, rough cloaks, staffs, faces lit from above in the announcement.
# GROUP — THE MAGI: three travel-stained scholars of the east in layered deep-colored robes, star charts and gifts, arriving at a HOUSE (Matt 2:11), the child a toddler — never at the manger.

### The Twelve — individual profiles (Peter, John the beloved, and Judas above)

# CAST — ANDREW: Physical — Peter's younger brother, leaner build, shorter beard, the same fisherman's weathering, a satchel always ready. Personality — the introducer: brings Peter (John 1:42), the boy with loaves (John 6:8-9), the Greeks (John 12:22); content to connect and step aside. Aliases — "Peter's brother", "the introducer".
# PIXAR — ANDREW: Want — to bring people to Him | Silhouette — a guiding hand on someone else's shoulder, presenting | Trust-watch — invisibility; every episode he appears, he MUST bring someone.

# CAST — JAMES (son of Zebedee): Physical — John's older brother, broad-shouldered, storm-dark brows, a boat-hand's rope burns. Personality — a "son of thunder" (Mark 3:17) who wants fire from heaven (Luke 9:54) and learns the cup he'll drink (Mark 10:39). Aliases — "the elder brother", "son of thunder".
# PIXAR — JAMES: Want — the kingdom NOW, by force if needed | Silhouette — squared stance, half a step ahead of John | Trust-watch — merging into John; thunder differentiates them.

# CAST — PHILIP: Physical — a practical Galilean with a counter's squint, neat beard, fingers that tally. Personality — the calculator: "two hundred denarii would not be enough" (John 6:7); "show us the Father" (John 14:8); finds Nathanael with "come and see" (John 1:46). Aliases — "the calculator".
# PIXAR — PHILIP: Want — things to add up | Silhouette — counting on fingers before wonder stops him | Trust-watch — doubt-caricature; his math makes the miracles land harder.

# CAST — NATHANAEL (Bartholomew): Physical — a thoughtful young man often found under a fig tree, scholar's sash over a worker's tunic, honest unguarded face. Personality — "can anything good come from Nazareth?" then the season's fastest confession (John 1:46-49); an Israelite with no deceit. Aliases — "Bartholomew", "the man under the fig tree".
# PIXAR — NATHANAEL: Want — the real thing, no pretending | Silhouette — rising from under the fig tree | Trust-watch — he carries the ladder echo (John 1:51) — never give that beat to anyone else.

# CAST — MATTHEW: Physical — softer hands than the fishermen, a once-fine robe gone plain, ink-stained fingers, eyes that expect contempt and keep finding welcome. Personality — left the tax booth mid-count (Matt 9:9); threw the feast that scandalized the righteous (Matt 9:10); the recorder who remembers. Aliases — "Levi", "the tax collector".
# PIXAR — MATTHEW: Want — to belong at the table he never deserved | Silhouette — pen stilled over a ledger, looking up | Trust-watch — redeemed means WELCOMED, not scrubbed; keep his old-life awkwardness at gatherings.

# CAST — THOMAS: Physical — a wiry realist with a furrowed brow, travel cloak always half-packed, hands that need to touch to trust. Personality — braver than his reputation ("let us also go, that we may die with him" — John 11:16); asks the question everyone has (John 14:5); his honest doubt becomes the Gospels' highest cry: "My Lord and my God" (John 20:28). Aliases — "the twin", "the honest doubter".
# PIXAR — THOMAS: Want — proof he can hold | Silhouette — reaching hand, hesitating one beat | Trust-watch — "doubting Thomas" mockery is banned; his doubt is the audience's doorway.

# CAST — JAMES (son of Alphaeus): Physical — small-framed and quick, young, a listener at the circle's edge. Personality — the quiet faithful; scripture keeps his deeds private and his presence constant. Aliases — "James the younger".
# CAST — THADDAEUS (Jude): Physical — round warm face, patched cloak, a brother's ease. Personality — asks the supper-table question "why show yourself to us and not the world?" (John 14:22). Aliases — "Jude".
# CAST — SIMON THE ZEALOT: Physical — lean and coiled, an old fighter's scar, the zealot's sash now worn under a disciple's plain robe. Personality — the revolutionary who sits at one fire with a tax collector — the kingdom's living proof. Aliases — "the zealot".
# PIXAR — THE QUIET THREE (James the younger, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot): Want — to stay faithfully in the story | Silhouette — the steady middle of every group frame | Trust-watch — Simon-and-Matthew seated TOGETHER is a recurring deliberate image; never waste it.

### The Saved and the Healed
(Cluster rule — the affliction is never the spectacle; the meeting is the
shot. Every healed face returns later in a crowd, visibly whole: the registry
makes gratitude continuity free.)

# CAST — NICODEMUS: Physical — an elderly ruling teacher, rich dark robes with scholar's tassels, a lantern in hand by night (John 3:2), careful eyes learning wonder. Personality — comes by night with questions, defends by day with law (John 7:50), carries myrrh at the end in open daylight (John 19:39). Aliases — "the teacher who came by night".
# PIXAR — NICODEMUS: Want — understanding without cost | Need — to step into daylight | Silhouette — lantern raised at a doorway | Trust-watch — his arc spans the whole season (night → dusk → daylight); plant each stage.

# CAST — ZACCHAEUS: Physical — a small, quick, richly dressed chief tax collector, rings on soft fingers, surprisingly nimble in a sycamore tree. Personality — wants to SEE more than to be seen (Luke 19:3-4); joy makes him generous on the spot — half to the poor, fourfold restored (Luke 19:8). Aliases — "the little tax collector", "the man in the tree".
# PIXAR — ZACCHAEUS: Want — one glimpse | Need — to be seen and named | Silhouette — small figure out on a branch | Trust-watch — the comedy is WITH him, never at him; the laugh turns to a lump in the throat at "Zacchaeus, come down."

# CAST — THE SAMARITAN WOMAN: Physical — a strong-faced woman at a noon well, water jar on her hip, layered Samaritan robes, eyes braced for judgment that doesn't come. # CAST — THE THANKFUL LEPER: Physical — a Samaritan man, skin first shown mottled at a lawful distance (kid-gentle, never grotesque), then clean and shining; a returning runner. # CAST — THE HEALED WOMAN: Physical — a careworn woman wrapped in veils who has been sick twelve long years, reaching for a hem through forest-of-legs crowd (Luke 8:43-44); afterward upright, unveiled face full of morning. ### Named witnesses and figures of the Passion

# CAST — JOSEPH OF ARIMATHEA: Physical — a wealthy, grave council member in fine dark robes, silver-bearded, a rich man's bearing carrying a poor man's grief. Personality — a secret disciple who goes PUBLIC at the costliest hour, asking Pilate boldly for the body (Mark 15:43); gives his own new tomb (Matt 27:60). Aliases — "the rich councilman", "the man who gave his tomb".
# PIXAR — JOSEPH OF ARIMATHEA: Want — faith without exposure | Need — one brave public act | Silhouette — standing alone before Pilate's seat | Trust-watch — his courage lands BECAUSE Friday looks final; do not soften the risk.

# CAST — ANNAS: Physical — the old power behind the vestments — plainer robes than Caiaphas, sharper eyes, a questioner by lamplight (John 18:13). Personality — the first interrogation; influence without office. Aliases — "the elder priest". (Care rules of THE AUTHORITIES apply.)
# CAST — SIMEON: Physical — a very old man in the temple courts, trembling hands that have waited a lifetime, face like a lamp at oil's end. Personality — promised he would see; sings over the baby — "now let your servant depart in peace" (Luke 2:29-32). Aliases — "the old man in the temple".
# CAST — ANNA: Physical — a tiny ancient prophetess, eighty-four, bent but bird-bright, never leaving the temple (Luke 2:36-37). Personality — fasting, praying, and then telling EVERYONE (Luke 2:38). Aliases — "the prophetess in the temple".
# PIXAR — THE TEMPLE ELDERS (Simeon, Anna): Want — to see the promise before sleeping | Silhouette — ancient hands holding the infant | Trust-watch — this is the OT season's audience on screen — the ones who waited; let the Thread visibly touch them.

# CAST — ELIZABETH: Physical — a grey-haired mother long past hoping, aglow with impossible late joy, hands on a blessed belly (Luke 1:41). Personality — the first to bless Mary out loud; her baby leaps. Aliases — "the cousin".
# CAST — ZECHARIAH: Physical — an old priest struck silent, communicating in wide eyes and wax tablets (Luke 1:63), voice returning in a flood of prophecy. Personality — doubt, silence, then the Benedictus. Aliases — "the silent priest".
# CAST — HEROD THE GREAT: Physical — a heavy, aging king in too much gold, fear wearing a crown. Personality — "troubled, and all Jerusalem with him" (Matt 2:3); lies to the Magi. The flight to Egypt is shown; his cruelty is NEVER depicted — one hushed narrator line and the family already safe on the road. Aliases — "the fearful king".
# CAST — CLEOPAS: Physical — a road-weary disciple on the Emmaus way, sandals and sorrow, walking-stick conversation. Personality — "we had hoped…" (Luke 24:21); heart burning at opened scriptures; knows Him in broken bread. Aliases — "the Emmaus walker".
# GROUP — THE WOMEN OF THE DAWN: Mary Magdalene with Joanna, Salome and the other Mary — spice jars and dawn cloaks, the first congregation of the resurrection (Luke 24:1,10).
