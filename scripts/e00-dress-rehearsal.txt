# ARC — WANT: Eli wants to hear one more story | NEED: to learn the promise holds in the dark | TURN: the lamp flickers and holds | TEAR-BEAT: Naomi's "the light was always coming" (~70%)
# CAST — ABRAHAM: Physical — a vigorous old man of about one hundred, deeply lined warm face, long silver beard and hair, sun-browned Middle-Eastern skin, striped brown-and-cream woolen herdsman's robes with a woven belt and head-cloth. Personality — believes God against all evidence; the friend of God. Aliases — "the father", "the old man", "the friend of God".
# PLACE — THE LAMP ROOM: a cozy ancient night-time home interior, warm stone walls, a woven rug, a single glowing clay oil lamp on a low stone table at center, soft hearth-glow at one side, deep indigo night through one small window.
# NOTE — GOD: never depicted; warm light and the Voice alone.

NARRATOR: [[Palette: indigo and silver. Three-second cold open: extreme close-up of the lamp flame shrinking to a tiny blue bead in the dark — then a child's small hand reaches in with a fresh wick, and the flame climbs back golden.]] The flame nearly slept tonight... but someone fed it.
NAOMI: Little ones! The stars are out, the night is soft... so tell me — who lights the lamp?
ELI: WE light the lamp!
PIP: Ooooh... watch it glow!
NAOMI: Then gather close, lamplighters — tonight is a little story about remembering. Sing it with me!
ELI: Your word is a lamp for my feet!
PIP: A light for my path so sweet!
ELI: When the road is dark and long —
PIP: Light the lamp and sing along!
ELI: [excited] Naomi — tell the one about the dark night! When the promise had no proof yet!
NAOMI: [softly] Ah — then do you remember our friend Abraham, little ones — the old man who counted stars?
ELI: [awe] I remember! More stars than numbers!
NARRATOR: [[The registered Abraham under the deep star field, tiny beneath the immense sky, the same low-angle starlit face as the covenant night.]] Far away and long ago, Abraham stood beneath those very stars, and heard the Voice that never breaks a promise.
VOICE(atlas): I am your shield, Abraham. Count the stars — so shall your children be. And through your family, every family on earth will be blessed.
PIP: [softly] So many stars...
ABRAHAM(orion): [awe] Then I will trust You, Lord... even if I never live to see it.
NARRATOR: [[The Promise Thread: a thin glowing golden filament with a faint crimson edge drifts gently from the lamp toward the horizon — thin but steady, faintly brighter at this covenant moment — never touching the characters, always softer than the lamp's own light.]] And from that promise a golden thread began to run through every story that followed — thin as a hair, bright as hope.
NAOMI: [tender] And where was that thread running TO, little ones? Lean close. [[Floating golden dream-vignette glowing in the warm air above the clay lamp: Jesus — deep warm tan, sun-browned Middle-Eastern skin, dark wavy hair and beard, simple cream robes, a calm kind loving face — holding/with a small white lamb cradled gently in His arms, soft golden radiance all around Him; the fireside cast gaze up at it in wonder.]]
NAOMI: To Him. Every star, every promise, every story — the light was always coming.
PIP: [excited] I saw it! I saw where the thread goes!
NAOMI: Quiz time, lamplighters! What did God tell Abraham to count? Shout it out!
ELI: The STARS! More stars than numbers!
NAOMI: Lamp down low. Say it slow. So your heart will always know.
NAOMI: Your word is a lamp to my feet, and a light to my path. Psalm one hundred nineteen, verse one hundred five.
ELI: A lamp to my feet.
PIP: A light to my path!
NAOMI: Next time, little ones — the very beginning: darkness, a breath... and light. Keep your lamps burning.
NARRATOR: Follow for the next lamplight story.
