# ========== CAST PROFILES — scripture-grounded production notes (never spoken) ==========
# CAST — ABRAHAM: Physical — a vigorous old man of about one hundred, deeply lined warm face, long silver beard and hair, sun-browned Middle-Eastern skin, striped woolen robes of a wealthy herdsman with a woven belt and head-cloth (rich in livestock, silver and gold — Gen 13:2; "old, well advanced in years" — Gen 24:1). Personality — believes God against all evidence (Gen 15:6); runs to welcome strangers and serves them his best (Gen 18:2-8); a generous peacemaker who gives others first choice (Gen 13:8-9); prays boldly for others (Gen 18:23-32); honest about his fears and failures (Gen 12:12-13); obeys at once, rising early in the morning (Gen 22:3). Aliases — "the father", "the old man", "her husband", "the friend of God".
# CAST — SARAH: Physical — a beautiful woman of about ninety, beauty that age never erased (Gen 12:11,14), silver hair braided beneath a fine woven head-veil, laugh-lines around bright attentive eyes, graceful robes in warm desert colors. Personality — a quick dry wit that laughs at the impossible (Gen 18:12); listens at tent doors; fiercely protective; her guarded doubt melts into overflowing public joy (Gen 21:6 — "God has made laughter for me"). Aliases — "the mother", "his wife", "the old woman".
# CAST — ISAAC (the boy): Physical — a sturdy, cheerful boy of about twelve with dark curls and his mother's bright eyes, simple cream tunic and sandals, strong enough to carry the firewood up the mountain (Gen 22:6). Personality — trusting and observant; asks the honest question others fear to ask (Gen 22:7); stays beside his father without pulling away. In the birth scenes he appears ONLY as a tiny swaddled newborn — the twelve-year-old design applies from the pasture scenes onward. Aliases — "the boy", "his son", "the child".
# CAST — MELCHIZEDEK: Physical — a tall stately king-priest of Salem, deep bronze skin, long dark beard streaked with silver, layered white-and-gold priestly robes with a simple gold circlet, carrying bread and a cup of wine (Gen 14:18). Personality — serene royal warmth; blesses before he asks anything; priest of God Most High. Aliases — "the king of Salem", "the priest".
# GROUP — THE THREE VISITORS: three travelers in simple cream-and-ochre robes, calm ageless faces, a faint warmth of light about them as if they carry noon inside (Gen 18:2); they arrive, stand and move together.
# PROP — THE CARAVAN: one long line of laden donkeys and camels, bleating flocks, drovers with staffs, Abraham and Sarah at its head — the same caravan in every traveling shot.
# PROP — THE RAM IN THE THICKET: a full-grown ram with great curled horns and cream-colored wool, standing CAUGHT BY ITS HORNS in a dense green thorn-thicket, head held fast among the branches (Gen 22:13). Every time the ram appears — in the story, in a recap, in the quiz — it is this same ram, horns visibly tangled in the same thicket.
# PROP — THE ALTAR: rough unhewn desert stones stacked waist-high to a man (Ex 20:25), on a bare mountaintop.
# PROP — THE STAR FIELD: stars beyond counting stretched from one horizon to the other (Gen 15:5; 22:17).
# NOTE — ISAAC'S AGE: in every mountain scene Isaac is exactly the sturdy twelve-year-old boy of his profile — never younger, never grown.
# NOTE — GOD: never depicted as a person or figure; His presence is warm light from heaven and the Voice alone.
