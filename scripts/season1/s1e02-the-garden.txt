# S1E02 — The Garden
# PROMISE: You will walk in the one place where nothing was broken — and feel what we were made for.
# REPEATABLE — "God walked WITH them!"
# ARC — WANT: Pip wants to know what the garden smelled like | NEED: to learn that the best thing in Eden was not the fruit but the Friend | TURN: the evening walk — God comes to THEM | TEAR-BEAT: "that is what you were made for" (~70%)
# CAST — ADAM: Physical — a strong young man in his first days, warm deep-tan skin, thick dark curly hair and short beard, simple garment of woven leaves, bright unguarded eyes. Personality — wonder-filled namer of creatures (Gen 2:19-20); works the garden with joy (Gen 2:15). Aliases — "the man", "the first man".
# CAST — EVE: Physical — a beautiful young woman, warm tan skin, long flowing dark hair, graceful and strong, garment of woven leaves, eyes quick with delight. Personality — curious and brave-hearted; the mother of all living (Gen 3:20). Aliases — "the woman", "the mother of all living".
# CAST — THE SERPENT: Physical — an elegant gleaming serpent of burnished copper-gold scales moving upright among branches, beautiful and subtly wrong, never horror. Personality — smooth, flattering, question-twisting (Gen 3:1). Aliases — "the serpent", "the snake".
# PLACE — THE LAMP ROOM: a cozy ancient night-time home interior, warm stone walls, a woven rug, a single glowing clay oil lamp on a low round stone table at center, soft hearth-glow at one side, deep indigo night through one small arched window.
# PLACE — THE GARDEN OF EDEN: a paradise garden of impossible green, a crystal river braiding through flowering meadows, fruit trees heavy with every color, soft golden light always at the hour before evening, two great trees at its heart.
# NOTE — GOD: never depicted; warm light and the Voice alone.

NARRATOR: [[Palette: morning gold on endless green. Three-second cold open: a single dewdrop on a leaf, the whole young world curved inside it — then the drop falls, and we are flying low over the garden of Eden, the crystal river flashing beneath.]] Do you remember the world arriving at one Voice? Light, and land, and every living thing? Tonight we go to the heart of it all... the garden.
NAOMI: Little ones! The stars are out, the night is soft... so tell me — who lights the lamp?
ELI: WE light the lamp!
PIP: Ooooh... watch it glow!
NAOMI: Then gather close, lamplighters — tonight we walk where nothing was broken yet. Breathe deep. Sing it with me!
ELI: Your word is a lamp for my feet!
PIP: A light for my path so sweet!
ELI: When the road is dark and long —
PIP: Light the lamp and sing along!
NAOMI: Last time, little ones, God made people in His own image — the best for last. Tonight: where He put them, and why.
NARRATOR: [[Wide establishing: the garden of Eden in full glory, the river braiding silver through the green, fruit trees bending heavy; Adam and Eve walking its paths like children in their father's house.]] God planted a garden in the east, in Eden, and there He put the man and the woman. Trees beautiful to see and good for food. A river to water everything. And work to do — real, happy work.
PIP: [excited] Work can be HAPPY?
NAOMI: In Eden it was, little lamb — treasure-box time! The Bible says God put Adam in the garden "to work it and to keep it." Before anything ever went wrong, work was a GIFT — like tending your own little lamp. Hard things came later; work itself was part of paradise.
NARRATOR: [[Joyful montage: Adam laughing as creatures parade past — a lion rubbing his shoulder like a cat, a heron stalking by, a long-eared hare; Eve kneeling nose-to-nose with a fawn.]] And God brought the animals to Adam to see what he would name them. Lion. Sparrow. Honeybee. Whatever Adam called each one — that was its name.
ELI: [awe] HE got to name them? All of them?
NAOMI: Every one, Eli — and remember last week: naming means knowing and caring. God shared His own naming-joy with the man. That is how much He trusted him.
NARRATOR: [[Golden-hour close: Adam and Eve together at the river's edge, bare feet in the shallows, utterly unafraid.]] And the man and the woman were naked and unashamed — nothing to hide, nothing to fear, no shadow anywhere between them, or between them and God.
NARRATOR: [[Palette: the hour before evening — the light turns honey-colored; a gentle golden brightness moves through the trees like someone walking, leaves bowing softly as it passes.]] And then — listen — the best part of every day. In the cool of the evening... God Himself came walking in the garden.
NAOMI: [tender] Hold your lamp very still, little ones. The Maker of stars came down the garden path — to walk with THEM. To talk about the day. The way your mother sits on your bed at night. THAT is what you were made for. Not just to live in His world... but to walk with Him in it.
PIP: [softly] He wanted... company?
NAOMI: He wanted THEM, little lamb. And He wants you.
ELI: The lion and the lamb would play —
PIP: the river sang the live-long day!
ELI: And when the evening light came through —
PIP: God came walking — just to be with you!
NARRATOR: [[The two great trees at the garden's heart: one radiant with golden fruit — the tree of life; beside it the other, beautiful and solemn, its fruit shadowed.]] Now, in the middle of the garden stood two trees. The tree of life... and one other. And about that one, God gave His children one clear, loving word.
VOICE(atlas): Of every tree of the garden you may freely eat. But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, you shall not eat — for in the day you eat of it, you will surely die.
ELI: ONE rule? Out of the whole garden... just one?
NAOMI: Just one, Eli. A whole world of YES... and one loving NO. And here is tonight's clear thing: that rule was not a wall to keep them out of something good. It was a fence at the top of a cliff. God's rules are always love wearing work clothes.
NARRATOR: [[Quiet beat: Adam and Eve walking away hand in hand through the gold; the camera holds on the solemn tree a moment longer — and low among its branches, half-hidden in soft focus, a gleam of copper-gold coils, elegant and still.]] And the man and the woman were happy — truly happy. But in the branches of that one tree... something gleamed. Something was watching. Patient... and smooth... and not yet ready to speak.
PIP: [softly] Naomi... what IS that?
NAOMI: Next week, little lamb. Hold your flame.
NAOMI: Quiz time, lamplighters! Who came walking in the garden in the cool of the day? Shout it out!
ELI: GOD did! He walked WITH them!
NAOMI: And how many trees were off-limits in the whole wide garden? Shout it out!
PIP: ONE! Just one — a fence of love!
NAOMI: Lamp down low. Say it slow. So your heart will always know.
NAOMI: I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Psalm one hundred thirty-nine, verse fourteen.
ELI: Fearfully and wonderfully made.
PIP: Wonderfully made!
ELI: Your word is a lamp for my feet —
PIP: a light for my path so sweet!
ELI: Follow the light!
PIP: Follow the light!
NAOMI: Next time, little ones — the gleam in the tree finds its voice, and asks the most dangerous question ever asked... and God makes the first promise of the whole long story. Bring your bravest hearts.
NARRATOR: [[End card grammar: the lamp close and warm, the night deep behind it.]] Follow for the next lamplight story.
